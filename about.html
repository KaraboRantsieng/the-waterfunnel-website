Our Story

Our Story

The WaterFunnel was established in 2020 with the aim to provide clean, reliable, high-quality hydration solutions (non-alcoholic) to the wider Vaal community, the rest of Gauteng, and beyond. We believe hydration should be simple, accessible, and enjoyable — whether it's purified still, sparkling, alkaline or flavoured water, fruit juices, ice, or water sachets; and other non-alcoholic beverages.

While our foundation lies in water purification and supply as our primary product, our long-term goal has always been broader: to become a leading supplier and distributor of high-quality hydration solutions.

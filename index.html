Our Story

Established in 2020, The WaterFunnel provides clean, reliable, high-quality hydration solutions across South Africa — from purified and alkaline water to refreshing juices, ice, and more.

About Us

The WaterFunnel is a trusted wellness‑focused beverage brand offering premium Purified Water and Alkaline Water as our core products, produced through advanced purification and pH‑balancing processes to deliver clean, healthy hydration.

Complementing our water range is our selection of refreshing fruit juices, crafted from quality ingredients sourced locally, to provide flavour-rich refreshments. Through our refill model, sustainable packaging options, and commitment to freshness, TWF provides complete hydration and refreshment for households, businesses, and events.

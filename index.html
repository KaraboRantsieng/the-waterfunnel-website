Who We Are

About The WaterFunnel

The WaterFunnel is a local beverage supplier proudly based in Meyerton, Gauteng. We supply purified and alkaline water as well as a full range of refreshing drinks — from fruit juice and iced tea to slush puppies, ice cream, and yoghurt.

Our mission is simple: provide clean, safe, and refreshing drinks for families, businesses, and the community. Every product we offer reflects our commitment to quality, hydration, and freshness.
